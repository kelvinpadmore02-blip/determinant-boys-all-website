OUR STORYLIBERIA

Football gives us a reason to grow together.

Determine Boys All Star is a football team from Liberia committed to developing football talent and creating an environment where players can work toward their potential.

Our club identity is grounded in teamwork, discipline and hard work. We believe improvement comes from showing up, learning together and giving our best to the team.

Club History: Determinant Boys All Star was founded in 2022 and based in STEPHEN A TOLBERT ESTATE, Determinant Boys All Star was established with a vision to promote football talent, teamwork,discipline, and among young people .
